THYNK is an program for Grades 3–8 that fuses India's Vedic Maths heritage with modern coding. Instead of teaching the two side by side, we use one to teach the other — every Vedic sutra becomes a doorway into a real coding concept, and every coding project reinforces mental maths. It's delivered as a structured, ready-to-teach curriculum, so your school doesn't need to hire or train specialist coding staff — just a room, a set of laptops, and children ready to build.

THE CORE IDEA
Why future-ready learning?
Can a child THINK, SOLVE, CREATE & ADAPT?

Children are growing up in a world shaped by AI, automation, digital technology and constant innovation. Future readiness is not just knowing technology — it is knowing how to think with it.

By 2030, every job will have a digital component. A child who cannot code in 2030 will be at the same disadvantage as a child who could not read English in 2000.

Every sutra your students already memorise for speed maths has a direct, one-to-one equivalent in programming logic — so coding class becomes reinforcement of what they know, not a brand-new subject from zero.

CURRICULUM CONNECTION

01

Builds on what you already teach

All schools introduce Vedic Maths in middle school — THYNK deepens that foundation into something practical and modern, with no new textbook to introduce and no existing lesson plan to disrupt.

02

Meets the NEP 2020 mandate

NEP 2020 requires coding from Grade 6. THYNK gives a structured, ready-to-deliver way to fulfil this without hiring specialist coding teachers, backed by a documented curriculum path your school can present at audit.

03

3–5× faster mental computation

Vedic techniques boost mental calculation speed, leading to faster debugging, stronger logic and greater coding confidence — an edge that shows up well beyond the coding classroom.

04

Pride in India's mathematical heritage

Ancient Vedic Maths meets modern coding, empowering confident learners who see coding not as something borrowed from abroad, but as an extension of a tradition they already own.
